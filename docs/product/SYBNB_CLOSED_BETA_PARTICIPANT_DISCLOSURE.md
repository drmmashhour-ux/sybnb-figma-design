## What the beta is

SYBNB is running a **managed closed beta**. It is a controlled, hand-operated trial — not the final
product. Things work, but some steps are done manually by our team.

## Scope — Daily Stays (STR) only

Only the **Daily Stays (short-term rental)** section is active. Every other section — Rentals, Buy, Cars,
Marketplace, New Construction, Sell, and Ride — is shown as **"Soon"** and is **not available** in the
beta.

## Payments are reviewed by hand

- You pay using the disclosed method and **submit a payment proof**.
- Our team **reviews the proof manually**. Your booking is **confirmed only after the proof is approved** —
  submitting a proof is not instant confirmation.
- You will receive **email updates** on your booking and payment status where an email is on file.

## Booking lookup

There is **no full guest account** in the beta. To check your booking, use **booking lookup** with your
**confirmation number and phone number**. This is the official way to find your trip.

## Host payouts are manual

- If you are a host, register your payout destination in the app.
- Payouts are **sent by hand** by our team on a schedule of **`<e.g. weekly>`**. They are **not instant**
  and **not automated**.
- We record each payout with a reference; funds are only marked completed once actually sent.

## Unpaid holds expire

- Starting a booking holds the dates temporarily. If payment is not completed, the **unpaid hold
  eventually expires** and the dates are released so others can book. This is normal — you can simply book
  again. An expired hold is **not** a penalty.

## Known limitations

- **No guaranteed automation** — payments, payouts, and much of support are manual.
- **STR only** — other sections are not available.
- **No guest dashboard** — use booking lookup + email + support.
- **Email delivery is best-effort** — if you don't receive an email, use booking lookup or contact
  support. Your booking and payment status are always the real record regardless of email.

## Privacy and data handling (summary)

- We collect only what is needed to operate your booking (contact details, payment proof, and — for
  identity verification — the documents you choose to upload).
- Private documents (identity/payment proofs) are stored privately, served as downloads (not shown
  inline), and staff access to them is audited.
- **Storage location note:** during the beta, uploaded files are stored in Cloudflare R2 with an
  **Eastern Europe** data placement. This is **not** the formal EU-jurisdiction data-residency boundary;
  a true EU-jurisdiction setup is planned before any public launch.
- We do not sell your data. For questions about your data, contact support.
